My work bridges classical multi-view geometry and neural scene representations — building calibrated, geometry-aware pipelines that span photogrammetric point clouds, UAV LiDAR, and dynamic 4D reconstruction using Gaussian Splatting and Neural Radiance Fields. I am driven by a desire to develop immersive, scientifically meaningful digital models that improve our understanding of the physical world, particularly in the context of urban digital twins and their applications to global challenges.

Research

4D Gaussian Splatting for dynamic scene reconstruction

Developing a 4D Gaussian Splatting framework for spatio-temporal scene reconstruction from synchronized multi-view video. Includes an in-house multi-camera data acquisition setup using GoPro Hero 13 cameras and a calibrated pose-estimation pipeline using COLMAP as a principled alternative to monocular initialization. Current work at ITU Copenhagen.

Photogrammetric point clouds for 3D GIS modeling (PhD thesis)

Thesis ↗

Introduced a novel approach for creating 3D GIS models from UAV photogrammetric point clouds, developing pipelines for semantic extraction, LOD3 building reconstruction, and query-based simulation. Integrated UAV imagery, low-cost LiDAR, and open-source photogrammetric tools into scalable geospatial workflows. IIT Roorkee, 2018–2024.

Open-source photogrammetry: advancements and benchmarking

Paper ↗

Systematic review and benchmarking of open-source photogrammetric pipelines from a point cloud standpoint — evaluating reconstruction fidelity, scalability, and practical applicability across Pix4D, Metashape, and open-source alternatives for close-range and UAV applications.

About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Audio-Visual Computing Group, IT University of Copenhagen, where I work on 4D Gaussian Splatting and spatio-temporal feature extraction from multi-view video. My research trajectory spans over a decade — from architectural design and UAV photogrammetry through point cloud processing and 3D GIS, to neural rendering and dynamic scene reconstruction.

I completed my PhD at IIT Roorkee (2018–2024) on photogrammetric point clouds for 3D GIS modeling, and hold an M.Tech in Geomatics Engineering and a B.Arch from NIT Patna. This unusual combination — architecture, geomatics, and computer vision — grounds my work in geometric and spatial reasoning rather than purely learned representations.

I supervise MSc theses at ITU Copenhagen, review for journals including ISPRS and Applied Earth Observation, and have organized and presented at international conferences across Europe, Asia, and Australia. I am actively pursuing postdoctoral and faculty opportunities in computer vision, 3D scene understanding, and neural rendering.
