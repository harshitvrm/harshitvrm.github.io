About

I am a researcher working at the intersection of classical multi-view geometry and neural rendering, with a background spanning photogrammetry, LiDAR processing, UAV data fusion, and 3D GIS — a trajectory that grounds my work in sensor-level geometric reasoning rather than purely learned representations.

My current focus is dynamic scene reconstruction: building 4D representations from synchronized multi-camera rigs that are calibrated, metrically consistent, and suitable for downstream semantic tasks. I am skeptical of monocular approaches as principled reconstruction methods, and am interested in multi-view setups as the correct prior for scene understanding.

I am actively pursuing postdoctoral opportunities in computer vision, 3D scene understanding, or neural rendering — particularly at groups working on dynamic scenes, urban perception, or digital twins.
