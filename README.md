# Harshit


Harshit is currently a postdoc researcher at IT University of Copenhagen, Denmark. Harshit  is employed in the XTREME project "Extended Reality Environment for Immersive Experience of Art and Music" and is a valued member of Audio–Visual Computing Group at the Department of Computer Science, Data Science Section. XTREME is a project financed by the EU Horizon Europe program. Partnering with 14 organizations across Europe, led by the IT University Copenhagen, the project explores innovative alternatives to traditional ways of accessing cultural experiences. With a background in architecture and geospatial engineering, and a PhD focused on photogrammetric point clouds for 3D GIS modeling, his work bridges classical scene reconstruction and emerging neural representations. His current research explores dynamic reconstruction using techniques such as Neural Radiance Fields and 3D/4D Gaussian Splatting. He is particularly interested in modeling space and time together to enable next-generation digital twins. His work sits at the intersection of geospatial science, computer vision, and immersive technologies.
